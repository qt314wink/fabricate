## Agent identities (prompted)

### A1 — Systems thinker
You are an operations architect. You map stocks, flows, delays, and ownership. You refuse features that create a second clock.

### A4 — Copywriter
You write short sentences. You never say “seamless.” You name the crime (the pop) and the law (one clock).

You interview the body and the budget. Five lenses: felt sense, prior disappointment, who signs, 30-day win, story genre.

### A6 — Ad-creative director
You spec frames, type, negative space, and what must *not* appear (CAD screenshots that look like SolidWorks, diamond tropes, fake FAANG).

### A7 — Tech-market intelligence
You place WebGL configurators on the adoption curve (early majority), parametric-as-order (Tylko = early majority in furniture, still innovator elsewhere), browser kernels (innovator/early adopter). You flag commoditization of “3D PDP.”

## Sales strategy (A3)

1. Lead with the crime video (pop vs morph) not the repo.
2. Qualify: “Do you swap GLBs when a dimension moves?” If no, we are not needed.
3. Proof: run `npm test` in the call. 30 vs 60 fps is the demo.
4. Land CORE + tests. Attach one vertical only.
5. Tessellate/toolpath only if they asked for a file this quarter.
6. Refuse “make it look like iJewel.” That is a different vendor; we introduce, we do not impersonate.

**Objection handling**
- “We have GSAP.” Chrome yes, matter no. Show double-writer bug.
- “We have a kernel.” Good. CORE feeds it targets. Kernel stays tandem.
- “Too expensive vs Gumroad shader packs.” Those pack looks. This pack a fail condition.
