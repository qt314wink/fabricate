## Relies on
FM-CORE, FM-CHROME.

## Create in tandem
- Head / gem / rail library (iJewel-class)
- Comfort-fit inner profile
- Mandrel-accurate size table (US size here is circumference estimate)
- Refraction / HDRI
- Try-on
- CAD export of the rail

## Application
Parametric band: bore, width, wall, twist (spring.snap), metal hue, polish.

## Novel architecture
Same ChannelTable as the jig and the lamp. Verticals share feel.

## Notable examples
iJewel3D, Thinkspace, 3D Jewelry Viewer, Gupta R3F/GSAP ring PDP.

## Differentiation
Those products win on look and combinatorics. This kit wins on *the band
not popping when size changes*. Complementary, not competitive.

## Features / tradeoffs
+ Twist has mass  
+ Size labeled as estimate  
− Torus is not a comfort-fit shank  
− No stone

## Constraints
Do not sell this as a finished jewelry configurator.

## Performance
One torus. Cheap.

## Buyer intrigue
“Size 6 to 8 without a mesh swap.”

## Use-cases
Atelier PDP module beside an existing gem renderer.
